## Prompts finaux

Chaque image a fait l’objet d’un appel distinct. Préfixe commun :

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium food ingredient photography for a French flavor discovery prototype. Natural editorial studio photography, refined realistic textures, soft directional daylight, subtle contact shadows, warm white to very pale sage backdrop, restrained botanical composition. High detail, vivid true-to-life ingredient colors, no harsh saturation. No lettering, labels, logos, packaging, watermarks, utensils or hands.

### hero-citrus

> Create one portrait 4:5 still-life photograph: freshly sliced bright green limes, one whole lime and a small yellow lemon, a few elegant natural citrus leaves on a pale warm-white stone surface. Macro focus on translucent juicy lime flesh and natural droplets, gentle depth of field. A dynamic but uncluttered arrangement fills the frame with tactile depth and plenty of softly lit space around the fruit. A calm premium sensory food mood, suitable for a large right-side website hero image.

### lemon

> Create one square catalog photograph of two natural yellow lemons, one whole and one freshly cut half showing translucent juicy segments, with one small citrus leaf. Three-quarter angle, carefully balanced central composition, fruit fills 70% of the frame.

### lime

> Create one square catalog photograph of two natural vivid green limes, one whole and one freshly cut half showing delicate juicy segments, with one small citrus leaf. Three-quarter angle, carefully balanced central composition, fruit fills 70% of the frame.

### grapefruit

> Create one square catalog photograph of a fresh pink grapefruit, one whole fruit behind a cleanly cut half revealing rich pink-red juicy flesh, with a small green leaf. Three-quarter angle, carefully balanced central composition, fruit fills 70% of the frame.

### vanilla

> Create one square catalog photograph of several slender textured dark bourbon vanilla pods, one split pod showing tiny vanilla seeds, and a single delicate cream-white vanilla orchid blossom. Three-quarter angle, carefully balanced central composition, ingredients fill 70% of the frame.

### strawberry

> Create one square catalog photograph of a small group of ripe red strawberries with fresh green calyxes, one strawberry cut in half showing its interior, naturally scattered with restrained elegance. Three-quarter angle, carefully balanced central composition, fruit fills 70% of the frame.
